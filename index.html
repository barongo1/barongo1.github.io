About Me & Academic Background

Blending three decades of frontline classroom leadership with postgraduate research in Artificial Intelligence in Education.

Professional Biography

I am a Senior Special Needs Education Teacher at Mathare Special Training Centre in Nairobi, Kenya, and an M.Ed. candidate in Technology in Education at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT).

Over my 30-year career, I have dedicated myself to fostering equity, Universal Design for Learning (UDL), and competency-based vocational education for learners with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD). My current research focuses on bridging the gap between cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED) and resource-constrained special education environments across Africa.

iCOMPASS Framework

Inclusive Collaborative Platform for AI-Supported Student Success: An Ethical AI-Driven Collaborative Assessment Framework for Inclusive Competency-Based TVET Education in Low-Resource Contexts.

1. AI in Education (AIED)
Ethical machine learning algorithms assisting educators in tracking learning trajectories and skill acquisition.
2. Learning Analytics
Formative performance data analysis providing actionable insights for individualized instruction.
3. Universal Design (UDL)
Multimodal representation and accessible assessment touchpoints for diverse cognitive needs.
4. Ethical AI Governance
Human-in-the-loop oversight, data privacy, fairness, and transparency for vulnerable learners.
5. Multi-Stakeholder Loop
Connecting instructors, learners, families, vocational mentors, and industry TVET employers.
