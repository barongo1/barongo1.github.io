# Career Timeline: Anne Barongo Ondieki

- **1992**: Joined Mathare Special Training Centre as Special Needs Education Teacher.
- **2003**: Graduated with Diploma in Special Needs Education from Kenya Institute of Special Education (KISE).
- **2007**: Served as Deputy Head Teacher at Mathare Special Training Centre, overseeing school management and curriculum coordination.
- **2015**: Graduated with Bachelor of Commerce (Finance) from University of Nairobi.
- **2019**: Enrolled in Master of Education (M.Ed.) in Technology in Education at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT).
- **2023–Present**: Conceptualized and developed the **iCOMPASS** framework (*Inclusive Collaborative Platform for AI-Supported Student Success*) for inclusive TVET competency-based assessment.
- **Present**: Senior Special Needs Education Teacher & Senior Master I at Mathare Special Training Centre; Lead Researcher on Ethical AI in Special Needs TVET Education.
