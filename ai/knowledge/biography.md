# Professional Biography: Anne Barongo Ondieki

## Executive Summary
**Anne Barongo Ondieki** is a distinguished Special Needs Education Specialist, Educational Technology Researcher, and Senior Educator with over 30 years of continuous service in inclusive education, educational leadership, and curriculum implementation in Kenya. She currently serves as a Senior Special Needs Education Teacher at Mathare Special Training Centre in Nairobi, while completing her Master of Education (M.Ed.) in Technology in Education at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT).

## Short Bio (Website Hero / Social)
Anne Barongo Ondieki is a Special Needs Education Specialist and Educational Technology Researcher investigating ethical AI-driven inclusive assessment frameworks for Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) institutions. With over three decades of frontline teaching and leadership at Mathare Special Training Centre, her research bridges artificial intelligence, learning analytics, Universal Design for Learning (UDL), and competency-based assessment to empower learners with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD).

## Medium Bio (Conference / Professional Profiles)
Anne Barongo Ondieki is a Senior Special Needs Education Teacher at Mathare Special Training Centre in Nairobi, Kenya, and an M.Ed. candidate in Technology in Education at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT). She holds a Bachelor of Commerce (Finance) from the University of Nairobi and a Diploma in Special Needs Education from the Kenya Institute of Special Education (KISE). 

Her research focuses on Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED), Learning Analytics, Universal Design for Learning (UDL), and Competency-Based Education (CBE). She is the principal researcher behind **iCOMPASS (Inclusive Collaborative Platform for AI-Supported Student Success)**, an ethical AI-driven assessment framework designed to personalize competency-based assessment for learners with intellectual and developmental disabilities in low-resource TVET contexts.

## Full Professional Biography
Anne Barongo Ondieki has dedicated over 30 years to advancing inclusive education, teacher mentoring, and educational leadership in Kenya. Starting her career in 1992, Anne has worked extensively with learners with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD), designing Individualized Education Programmes (IEPs), facilitating functional literacy and numeracy, and supervising vocational training initiatives.

Recognizing the transformational potential of technology in addressing systemic educational inequities, Anne pursued postgraduate research at JKUAT focusing on educational technology. Her current master's thesis and research project, **iCOMPASS**, addresses a critical research gap: the underrepresentation of learners with IDD in AI-supported assessment frameworks in low-resource African TVET institutions.

Her work advocates for human-in-the-loop, ethical AI governance that strengthens—rather than replaces—the central role of educators, while engaging families, instructors, and employers in data-informed, collaborative learner evaluation.
