# Teaching Philosophy & Pedagogy: Anne Barongo Ondieki

## Core Teaching Philosophy
> *"I believe every learner has the ability to succeed when teaching is inclusive, evidence-based, and responsive to individual needs. Technology, including Artificial Intelligence, is a tool for enhancing—not replacing—effective teaching by providing personalized support, timely feedback, and accessible learning opportunities."*

---

## 8 Pillar Instructional Framework
1. **Equity & Accessibility**: Creating barrier-free learning environments adapted to cognitive and physical abilities.
2. **Universal Design for Learning (UDL)**: Integrating multiple means of representation, expression, and engagement.
3. **Learner-Centered Pedagogy**: Tailoring instructional pace and learning goals around the unique strengths of each individual.
4. **Competency-Based Education (CBE)**: Focusing on mastery of practical, real-world vocational competencies rather than rigid time-bound testing.
5. **Individualized Education Programmes (IEPs)**: Formulating collaborative IEPs involving educators, parents, and multidisciplinary specialists.
6. **Ethical AI & Technology Integration**: Utilizing digital learning tools and ethical AI to provide timely formative feedback and scaffolded learning.
7. **Vocational Empowerment & Social Transition**: Preparing learners with intellectual disabilities for independent living, community participation, and meaningful employment.
8. **Refinement & Formative Analytics**: Continuously adjusting instructional strategies using empirical learning analytics and observation.
