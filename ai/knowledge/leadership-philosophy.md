# Educational Leadership Philosophy: Anne Barongo Ondieki

## Leadership Vision
Anne Barongo Ondieki views educational leadership as a collaborative, empowering endeavor grounded in mentorship, equity, and evidence-based innovation. Having served as Deputy Head Teacher (2007) and Senior Master I at Mathare Special Training Centre, her leadership approach centers on building institutional capacity for inclusive education.

## Core Leadership Principles
1. **Teacher Empowerment & Mentorship**: Supporting trainee teachers and peer educators through hands-on instructional coaching and ICT integration workshops.
2. **Inclusive Curriculum Governance**: Leading the transition to Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) and competency-based assessment models tailored to special needs contexts.
3. **Community & Stakeholder Alignment**: Strengthening partnerships between schools, families, healthcare professionals, vocational placement centers, and government bodies (e.g. Teachers Service Commission, Ministry of Education).
4. **Data-Informed Quality Assurance**: Utilizing educational analytics and structured IEP reviews to uphold high standards of special needs education.
