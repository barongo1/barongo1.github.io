# Research Overview & Flagship Project: Anne Barongo Ondieki

## Flagship Framework: iCOMPASS
**iCOMPASS** (*Inclusive Collaborative Platform for AI-Supported Student Success*) is an ethical AI-driven collaborative assessment framework designed to enhance competency-based assessment for learners with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) in Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) institutions operating in low-resource contexts.

### Research Context & Gap
While Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED) is expanding rapidly worldwide, learners with intellectual disabilities in developing nations remain significantly underrepresented. Traditional paper-based or manual competency assessment models in TVET institutions often lack:
- Timely, adaptive feedback for instructors and learners.
- Personalization tailored to non-standard cognitive development paces.
- Data-informed decision-making for individualized education programmes (IEPs).

### Core Pillars of iCOMPASS
1. **Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED)**: Utilizing ethical machine learning algorithms and multi-agent AI tools to assist educators in tracking learning trajectories and skill acquisition.
2. **Learning Analytics**: Analyzing formative performance data to generate actionable insights for personalized instruction.
3. **Universal Design for Learning (UDL)**: Ensuring multiple means of representation, action, expression, and engagement across all assessment touchpoints.
4. **Ethical AI Governance**: Enforcing human-in-the-loop oversight, data privacy, algorithmic fairness, transparency, and explainability tailored to vulnerable learner populations.
5. **Collaborative Assessment Ecosystem**: Actively bridging five key stakeholder groups—Instructors, Learners, Families, Vocational Mentors, and Industry Employers.

### Multi-Agent Educational AI Architecture
iCOMPASS implements a multi-agent AI model (detailed in [`ai/knowledge/agents.md`](file:///home/ogilo/Projects/barongo1.github.io/ai/knowledge/agents.md)) featuring:
- **Instructor Co-Pilot Agent**: Assists teachers in IEP drafting, rubric scoring, and scaffolded interventions.
- **Learner Companion Agent**: Delivers adaptive, UDL-compliant multimodal assessment tasks.
- **Family Engagement Agent**: Converts performance data into plain-language updates for caregivers.
- **Vocational Placement Agent**: Matches learner competency profiles with industry TVET standards.

### Methodology
- **Research Design**: Convergent mixed-methods design.
- **Target Population**: Public TVET institutions and special training centres offering vocational programmes for learners with intellectual disabilities in Kenya.
- **Data Streams**: Quantitative engagement metrics, competency achievement rubrics, qualitative teacher interviews, and stakeholder focus group feedback.
